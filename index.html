OpenPIV Educational Site

Learn Particle Image Velocimetry (PIV) and how to use OpenPIV properly. These lessons combine explanations, runnable examples, tips, and pitfalls.

Start here

  • Educational PIV overview — what PIV is, synthetic example, basic run, and key tips.
  • 01 — Introduction to PIV — physics, imaging, particles, and what a PIV algorithm measures.
  • 02 — Basic PIV workflow — windows, overlap, dt, running OpenPIV, and reading results.
  • 03 — Multi-pass & window deformation — why multiple passes improve accuracy and gradients.
  • 04 — Validation & filtering — signal-to-noise, outlier detection, and replacing bad vectors.
  • 05 — Masking & preprocessing — static/dynamic masking, when and why to use them.
  • 06 — Tips, tricks & pitfalls — practical checklist for reliable PIV.

What you will learn

  • How PIV estimates velocity from image pairs and what assumptions it makes.
  • How to choose window size, overlap, search area, and time step.
  • How to validate, filter, mask, and smooth results without hiding errors.
  • How to interpret OpenPIV outputs and diagnose common problems.
